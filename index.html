      HeartGold / SoulSilver tips

      • Touch screen: the bottom screen is your stylus. Click it with the mouse (or tap on a touch device) for the Pokégear, Pokétch, Pokéathlon and menus.
      • Controls: open the emulator's control settings from its menu bar to see or remap the keyboard bindings.
      • Saving: save in-game from the Start menu as usual. While you're unlocked, the save syncs to your private storage within about 30 seconds (watch the status at the top right), and the last 20 versions are kept as backups.
      • Restarting: use ← Library then Play. It saves and syncs first, then boots the game cleanly. (The emulator's own Restart button is hidden because it can cause "A communication error has occurred".)
      • Won't work: the Pokéwalker (needs infrared hardware) and anything over Wi-Fi, including the GTS and wireless trading.
      • Slow? DS is the most demanding system here. Close other tabs and keep the window from being resized mid-game.

      In-game saves sync to your private storage whenever you're unlocked, so you can continue on another device. Save states (the emulator's quick-save slots) stay in this browser only.
