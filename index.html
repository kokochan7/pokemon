    HeartGold / SoulSilver tips

    • Touch screen: the bottom screen is your stylus. Click it with the mouse (or tap on a touch device) for the Pokégear, Pokétch, Pokéathlon and menus.
    • Controls: open the emulator's control settings from its menu bar to see or remap the keyboard bindings.
    • Saving: save in-game from the Start menu as usual. Save states and the emulator's save-file download are there too, so grab a backup now and then.
    • Won't work: the Pokéwalker (needs infrared hardware) and anything over Wi-Fi, including the GTS and wireless trading.
    • Slow? DS is the most demanding system here. Close other tabs and keep the window from being resized mid-game.
